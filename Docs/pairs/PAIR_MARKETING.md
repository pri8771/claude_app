## Shared context (agreed by both orchestrators)
Hindsight: local-only iOS app (SwiftUI/SwiftData, no backend, no accounts, no third-party deps). Pivoting from a heavy 4-step decision wizard to: core object = a "Call" (one falsifiable statement + confidence 51-99% no-default + resolve-by date), sealed immutable on save, capture = one sheet / typing + 3 taps. Decisions remain optional containers around Calls (not deleted). Resolution: Yes / No / Partly (never scored; statement-quality diagnostic) / Can't-resolve (excluded, tracked). Insights: per-call reveal (n=1), Brier score (n>=10), 3-bin calibration (n>=10/bin), directional claims (n>=30). Social: "Timestamped Receipt" — sealed card posted to X/LinkedIn at capture (platform timestamp = notary), reveal card quote-posts it; honest framing = social accountability, not cryptography. ICP tension: founders/operators (high status, low call volume) vs forecasting types (high volume). Known launch blockers: preselected-"Correct" verdict bug, demo-data title-matching deletion bug, fatalError on store-open, no test target, no accessibility.

**Positioning Statement**
Hindsight: the local-first app for turning uncertain forecasts into evidence. Capture your predictions with confidence levels, resolve them honestly, and share sealed timestamps to X/LinkedIn as proof of your thinking—no backend, no accounts, just your judgment and the math that tracks it.

**App Store Assets**
- Name: Hindsight
- Subtitle: Your Forecasts, Your Evidence
- Keywords:
  1. forecasting, decision-making, confidence
  2. prediction tracking, calibration, journal
  3. decision log, accountability, privacy

**5-Screenshot Storyboard**
1. *Hero*: "Make a Call" sheet—user typing a falsifiable statement (e.g., "Stock market down Q3 2024"), slider showing 72% confidence. Copy: "Your prediction. Your confidence. Your proof."
2. *Capture*: Three taps post-typing: confidence slider → resolve-by date → publish Receipt to X. Copy: "Sealed at capture. Timestamped on your timeline. The internet doesn't forget."
3. *Resolution*: Card showing the original Call, user selecting Yes/No/Partly outcome, Brier score calculated (if n>=10). Copy: "How right were you? Learn your pattern."
4. *Insights*: Dashboard with per-call revelation, calibration bins (if n>=10/bin), directional trends (if n>=30). Copy: "Your thinking, quantified. No guessing, just numbers."
5. *Privacy*: Closed padlock icon, device silhouette. Copy: "Everything lives on your phone. No cloud. No ads. No tracking."

**App Store Description (100 words)**
Stop guessing whether you're actually good at predicting. Hindsight turns each forecast into falsifiable evidence: state your Call (one clear statement), add your honest confidence level (51–99%), and set a resolve-by date. On that date, mark the outcome—Yes, No, Partly, or Can't resolve—and watch Hindsight calculate your Brier score. Share sealed timestamps to X or LinkedIn at capture as social accountability, then reveal them when the truth lands. All local, all on your device, zero backend. Designed for operators and forecasters: capture one Call at a time, see your calibration over dozens, spot your real blind spots.
