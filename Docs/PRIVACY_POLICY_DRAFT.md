Hindsight is a private decision journal. The current version is designed to work entirely on your
device. It does not require an account and does not send your journal entries to a Hindsight
server.

## Information stored by the app

Hindsight stores the decisions, options, predictions, notes, review dates, and outcome reviews that
you enter. This information is stored locally on your device using Apple’s on-device persistence
frameworks.

The app also stores a small number of local preferences, such as whether onboarding has been
completed, whether review reminders are enabled, and whether haptic feedback is enabled.

## Information Hindsight collects

The current version of Hindsight does not collect personal information, journal content,
identifiers, analytics, advertising data, location, contacts, health information, or payment
information. It does not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs and does not track you
across apps or websites.

## Notifications

If you enable review reminders, Hindsight schedules local notifications on your device. Permission
is controlled through iOS Settings. Notification text is generic and does not include your
decision, prediction, or journal content.

## Exporting and sharing

You can export your journal as JSON or PDF. Export files are created locally and remain under your
control. Data leaves the app only when you choose a destination through the iOS share sheet.
Hindsight does not receive a copy.

## Demo data

You may optionally add example decisions to explore the app. Demo entries are identified
separately from your entries and can be removed without deleting your own journal.

## Deleting your data

You can delete individual decisions or clear all Hindsight data from within the app. Deletion
removes the selected local records and cancels related local notifications. Deleting the app also
removes its local application data, subject to Apple’s device and backup behavior.

## Data security and backups

Hindsight relies on iOS application sandboxing and device security. Information may be included in
device backups according to your Apple and iCloud settings. No system can guarantee absolute
security, so protect access to your device and Apple Account.

## Children’s privacy

Hindsight is not directed to children under 13, and the app does not knowingly collect personal
information from children.

## Changes to this policy

This policy will be updated before Hindsight introduces any server, account, analytics, social, or
third-party data processing. The effective date above will change when the policy changes.
